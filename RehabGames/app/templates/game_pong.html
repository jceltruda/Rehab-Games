Pong

Move your arm up and down to control the paddle.

Therapy Focus: Vertical Shoulder Mobility

This exercise trains Shoulder Flexion and Extension (the up-and-down motion of your arm).

This movement is essential for strengthening the muscles that help you lift your arm, improving your ability to perform daily tasks like reaching for an object on a high shelf.
